What I built

My contribution to the original project, described at a safe level.

Portfolio reconstruction

I designed a serverless monitoring platform that collected public weather and emergency information, applied configurable geospatial filtering and presented relevant events through a global dashboard.

Data ingestionConnected public information sources through structured APIs
ProcessingUsed serverless workflows to classify and refresh alert data
Geospatial logicFiltered broad alert areas against configured monitoring zones
Web productBuilt the map, severity overview and grouped alert tables
AutomationPrepared targeted notification and ticketing workflows
OutcomeImproved early visibility of potentially disruptive events
